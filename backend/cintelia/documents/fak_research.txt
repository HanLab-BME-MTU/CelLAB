FAK Protein Research Summary
=============================

Focal Adhesion Kinase (FAK) is a 125 kDa non-receptor tyrosine kinase encoded by the PTK2 gene.

Key Functions:
- FAK localizes to focal adhesions where cells attach to the extracellular matrix
- Phosphorylation at Tyr397 creates a binding site for Src family kinases
- FAK regulates integrin-mediated signaling essential for cell migration
- FAK knockout mice show embryonic lethality

Clinical Significance:
- FAK overexpression correlates with poor prognosis in breast, colon, and ovarian cancers
- FAK inhibitors VS-4718 and defactinib are in clinical trials
- FAK promotes cancer metastasis through EMT regulation

Citations: Schaller MD (2010) Nature Reviews. Sulzmaier FJ (2014) Nature Reviews Cancer.
